**Complete citation audit — Living Alignment, medium edits**

Reviewed September 21, 2026. This audit extracted the 137 distinct citation keys from active, uncommented text in `pnas_paper medium edits.tex` and matched them against the generated bibliography. Every cited entry was independently looked up. Uncited entries in the shared bibliography were excluded.

**Result:** all 137 cited works were identified in publisher, proceedings, author/institutional, DOI-registry, or authoritative library records. I corrected or completed 24 entries and left 113 unchanged. No additional fictitious work was found after the earlier Brown replacement. “Identified” does not mean that every source’s metadata is internally consistent; the decisions below explain the relevant conflicts.

Checks covered authors/editor roles and order, titles, publication year and edition, venue/publisher, volume/issue, pages or article identifiers, and existing DOI/URL/ISBN fields where supplied. Author lists deliberately ending in “and others” were checked against the published prefix; valid truncation was not treated as an omission. Existing preprint citations were retained when accurate.

This is bibliographic verification, not a fresh cover-to-cover scientific review of all 137 works. The previous scientific review checked claims and selected sources, so it did not already provide this complete audit. No manuscript prose was changed in this pass. The corrections are in `../assets/proxyfailure.bib`; citation keys were preserved.

**Source discrepancies and edition decisions**

- **Cunningham et al. (2024):** the published ICLR PDF gives Cunningham, Ewart, Riggs, Huben, Sharkey, matching this bibliography. The conference landing-page/index metadata uses a different ordering and one different surname. I retained the actual published PDF byline. The full audit row links the evidence.

- **CIRL (2016):** its publisher’s online BibTeX has no pages; conventional secondary citations give 3909–3917, whereas the printed proceedings contents use a different starting page. The optional page range was omitted, and the official online record is linked. This discrepancy is not evidence that the work is fictitious.

- **Books and online-first dates:** valid edition dates were preserved, including Haidt’s 2012 Vintage ebook, Maturana and Varela’s 2012 ebook, Sidanius and Pratto’s 2001 paperback, and Surowiecki’s 2005 Vintage edition. Issue years were retained when advance-online years differed. A citation key containing an old year is an internal identifier, not an error in the printed reference.

- **Bakan and Nagel:** the original files cannot establish which physical edition the authors consulted. Bakan now identifies a verified book edition consistent with the existing 2006 year. Nagel’s mismatched optional ISBN was removed. These choices preserve the existing cited years without inventing a claim about the consulted copies.

- **Access limits:** some publisher pages blocked direct retrieval. Original PDFs, publisher-deposited Crossref records, author repositories, official conference records, and library catalogs supplied alternative evidence. Historical website access-date notes were not independently reconstructible.
