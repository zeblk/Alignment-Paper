# PNAS revision notes

Ideas to revisit if we are invited to revise the paper, which is currently under review at PNAS.

## 16 October 2026

### Barriers and the messiness of life

I was looking at that AI wisdom paper again ([https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/abstract/S1364-6613(26)00002-1](<https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/abstract/S1364-6613(26)00002-1>)). I was mentally patting us on the back for telling a more general story about all of life, rather than just this one aspect of metacognition (which we mention in one of our sections as an example). But then I thought about how our paper is so abstract that I wonder if people will see how it's the same thing as life. Those abstract principles, like barriers and fixation, could sound almost mathematical and clean.

That led me to think, if we get a revision, we could emphasize more how barriers are exactly the reason why life is so gross. Any straight line or clean surface is a fixation. If evolution keeps recursively finding any possible collapse and replacing it with yet another layer of semi-independent perspective, the limit of that process is exactly what we perceive as organic/messy/biological.
